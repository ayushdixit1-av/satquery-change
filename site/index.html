How satellite intelligence works here

  1. Align — a phase-correlation pass confirms both frames view the same ground and reports a match score.
  2. Infer — a Siamese U-Net trained on remote-sensing imagery compares the pair (or reads the single scene) — in your browser, via ONNX + WebAssembly.
  3. Explain — an Otsu threshold isolates what changed, a cleanup pass drops speckle, and a live slider lets you tune the cut to your liking.
Built with PyTorch · ONNX · onnxruntime-web — no servers, no uploads, no tracking. Your imagery stays on your device.

How this demo works

  1. Align — a fast phase-correlation estimator checks whether the two images are the same scene and reports a match score.
  2. Infer — a Siamese U-Net change detector (trained on remote-sensing imagery) runs in your browser via ONNX + WebAssembly.
  3. Explain — an Otsu threshold finds a sensible split, tiny blobs are cleaned, and you can fine-tune the cutoff live.
Built with PyTorch → ONNX → onnxruntime-web · static, serverless, private
